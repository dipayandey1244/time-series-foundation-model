A field guide to models that learn reusable structure

Foundation models are learning to read, see and act in the world.

One family learns from sequences. One recognizes visual structure. A newer family tries to predict how a world changes when an agent acts. Their shared promise is reuse; their evidence and maturity are not the same.

Examples and availability checked October 2026 · deployment claims linked to their publishers.

Three foundation-model families

Different input, different output, different kind of question.

01 · TIME SERIES

Learn from what changes over time

history → future

Pretrained forecasters use past values to predict demand, energy load, traffic or telemetry. The output is a future trajectory, often with uncertainty.

p(yt+1:t+H | y1:t)

Examples: TimesFM · Chronos · Moirai

02 · VISION

Find structure in images and video

pixels → objects

Promptable vision models detect, segment and track objects. They can become reusable components in editing, visual search, scientific annotation and perception systems.

image / video + prompt → mask, label, track

Example: Meta Segment Anything (SAM)

03 · WORLD ACTION

Predict what an action will change

scene + action → outcome

World-action models couple a predictive model of physical scenes with action generation. The aim is to anticipate consequences, then plan or control—not just describe pixels.

p(next scene, action | observations, goal)

Emerging: NVIDIA Cosmos · research WAMs

Where they are showing up

Concrete uses, with the stage of adoption made explicit.

Time seriesForecasting in products and operations
Google Cloud exposes TimesFM through BigQuery’s AI.FORECAST; in its 2025 review, Google Research reported hundreds of millions of monthly TimesFM queries across BigQuery and AlloyDB. Decathlon selected Chronos-2 for weekly retail demand forecasts across products and supply zones, after testing it against its own production baseline. Google Research ↗ · AWS / Decathlon ↗
What it doesDemand, traffic and business-series forecasts for replenishment, planning and capacity. A pretrained model may still be adapted and validated on local data.
VisionRecognition and visual editing
Meta says SAM 3 and SAM 3D power Facebook Marketplace’s “View in Room” feature. With Conservation X Labs and Osa Conservation, Meta also released a wildlife-monitoring video dataset built using SAM 3. Meta · SAM 3 ↗
What it doesPrompts select and track objects across images or video; masks can support editing, measurement or human-reviewed annotation. It is a perception component, not a complete scientist or editor.
World actionRobotics and physical AI
NVIDIA is developing Cosmos models that connect scene understanding, future video and actions. Its Cosmos 3 Policy DROID demonstrations jointly predict robot action trajectories and future camera views; a Cosmos Edge guide describes post-training a manipulation policy for Jetson Thor. NVIDIA · WAMs ↗ · NVIDIA · robot policy ↗
What it doesImagine outcomes of actions to help train or guide robots. Today’s examples are research, developer workflows and bounded robot demonstrations—not evidence of general-purpose autonomous deployment.

A useful distinction: “foundation model” describes a training and reuse strategy, not proof of safe or reliable deployment. TimesFM and vision segmentation already have named product workflows; world-action models are newer and their evidence is mainly research and controlled demonstrations. Test every model on the target task.
